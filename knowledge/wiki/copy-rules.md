# Copy rules

- No diagnosis, no “you have X.”
- Lifestyle-level suggestions only (“commonly linked to … — discuss with your doctor”).
- Never invent a number in component code or Groq copy.
- Missing range → “range not available”. Missing benchmark → “benchmark data not yet available”.
- Reports keep a persistent, non-dismissible-on-first-view not-medical-advice disclaimer.
- Action-plan hover cues name a marker **and** a direction/grade (`given your higher LDL`), never a glued name (`EstradiolEstradiol`) and never a lab value.
- Do not hang every daily-routine line on a single hormone. Rotate flagged markers. Do not claim water “balances estradiol.”
